                Reading GA4 campaign names
                  Where the numbers come from
                  Metric definitions Every ratio is computed from summed numerators and denominators across the selected period, never by averaging monthly ratios.
                  TermDefinition
                  Paid spendMeta Ads + Google Ads spend as reported by each platform's API. GA4 contributes no spend, so nothing is double counted.
                  Platform-reported revenueEach network's own attributed conversion value, on its own attribution window. Shown as reported, never adjusted.
                  Platform ROASPlatform-reported revenue ÷ that platform's spend.
                  GA4 site revenueAll revenue GA4 recorded for the site, every channel included — paid, direct, organic and referral.
                  GA4-credited revenueRevenue GA4 attributes to a specific paid channel's sessions, using the strict source/medium rule below.
                  GA4-attributed ROASGA4-credited revenue ÷ that channel's spend.
                  Blended MERGA4 site revenue ÷ total paid spend. One denominator, so two platforms cannot inflate it by claiming the same order.
                  Attribution ratioTotal platform-claimed revenue ÷ GA4 site revenue. 1.0× would mean platform claims reconcile with analytics.
                  Revenue per sessionGA4 revenue ÷ sessions. Used in place of average order value, which this export cannot support.
                  GA4 key eventsGA4's conversions metric — every configured key event, not purchases. Never divided into revenue to produce an order value.
                  CTR / CPC / CPM / CPAClicks÷impressions, spend÷clicks, spend÷impressions×1000, spend÷conversions. All computed from summed totals.
                  Campaign verdictsScale, Hold, Fix, Cut and New, assigned by comparing each campaign to its own channel's blended ROAS. Thresholds are stated on the campaign page.
                  Known limitations Generated by the pipeline from the data it actually received.
                    Verification What was checked against the source systems, and what was not.
